# Where to Find "In-app Products" Section

## The Problem
You're on the **Distribution** page, but "In-app products" is on a **different page**.

## The Solution

1. **Click "Store listing" in the left sidebar** (under "Build" section)
2. Scroll down on the Store listing page
3. You'll find the **"In-app products"** section there

## Steps:

1. In the left sidebar, click **"Store listing"** (it's right above "Privacy" and below "Package")
2. Scroll down on that page
3. Look for a section called **"In-app products"** or **"Monetization"**
4. Click **"Add in-app product"** or **"Create in-app product"**
5. Fill in:
   - **Product ID:** `nimbus_yearly_subscription`
   - **Type:** Subscription
   - **Price:** £4.99
   - **Billing period:** Yearly (12 months)
   - **Title:** "Nimbus Yearly Subscription"
   - **Description:** "Unlock unlimited word definitions, synonyms, and examples for one year"

## Important:
- The "In-app products" section is on the **Store listing** page, NOT the Distribution page
- You must have "Contains in-app purchases" selected on Distribution (which you do ✅)
- But the actual product creation happens on Store listing
