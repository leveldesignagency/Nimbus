# Nimbus – Advertising Bullet Points

**Short tagline:** Highlighted = Enlightened

---

## One-liners & hooks
- Highlight any text. Get definitions, AI explanations, and translations in one click.
- Turn every webpage into a lesson. No switching tabs, no extra apps.
- Definitions, AI chat, translation, and maps—all from your selection.
- Your intelligent learning layer on top of the whole web.

---

## Feature bullets (for ads, landing pages, store)

### Core
- **Instant definitions** — Dictionary first, AI when it’s complex. Works for words, phrases, and full sentences.
- **AI explanations** — Highlight 3+ words for an AI summary; tap to copy and paste anywhere.
- **In-tooltip translation** — Pick “From” and “To” languages; long text opens Google Translate automatically.
- **Text-to-speech** — Natural voices for any selection. Built for pronunciation and listening.

### Smart detection
- **People, places, organisations** — Summaries, photos, Wikipedia, and recent news in the tooltip and in the Hub.
- **Addresses & places** — “View on Google Maps” and “Search on Google” directly from the tooltip.
- **Medical & technical terms** — Dedicated handling and optional Plain / Technical / Simple (ELI12) styles.

### Hub & organisation
- **Word of the Day** — One new word every day.
- **Favourites** — Save words and concepts to review later.
- **Recent searches** — Quick access to what you’ve looked up.
- **Conversations** — Revisit and continue AI chats.
- **Saved for Later** — Save whole pages (URLs) to read or study later.
- **Search bar** — Look up definitions, Wikipedia, and Google from the popup.

### Language & translation
- **16+ languages** — Definitions and UI in your language; flags-only picker in settings.
- **Translate from/to** — Choose source and target in the tooltip; long text (500+ chars) goes to Google Translate.
- **Auto-detect** — Uses your system language when possible.

### Privacy & flexibility
- **Local-first** — Favourites, recents, and settings stay on your device.
- **No tracking** — We don’t track browsing or collect personal data.
- **Incognito** — Works in private windows.
- **Optional AI** — Works out of the box with free APIs; add your OpenAI key for stronger AI.

## Audience bullets
- Students building vocabulary and revising.
- Professionals polishing communication and jargon.
- Language learners working on pronunciation and translation.
- Researchers quickly clarifying terms and entities.
- Anyone who reads online and wants to “highlight to understand.”

---

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Select** — Word, phrase, name, or address on any site.
2. **Get** — Definition, AI summary, translation, map, or news—depending on what you picked.
3. **Save** — Favourite words, save pages, and continue AI conversations in the Hub.
